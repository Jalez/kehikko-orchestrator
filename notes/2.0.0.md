- **A new Orchestrator: talk with your agent sessions in one place.** Sessions on its kehikko send their questions and progress reports here, and you answer a question by pressing one of its choices or typing.
- **Write to a session, a place or everyone.** What you send goes to the sessions as your own instruction and is typed into their terminals, once you turn on "Send messages to agent sessions as you" in its settings.
- **Questions in the Inbox.** Each unanswered question asks for your attention until you answer it, and the footer says how many wait and how many agents are working.
- **Sessions already running learn about it.** The Orchestrator introduces itself once to each session on its kehikko.
- **Starting sessions and reading transcripts are gone.** The agent modules start their own sessions, and their terminals show what they say.
